Complex Mediteranean 3*** Mamaia sits on the promenade in the northern part of the resort, roughly 50 metres from the beach, between the Modern and Rex hotels and 1 km from the Casino.

The complex has two buildings and offers pools for adults and children, terraces, bars and its own restaurant.
